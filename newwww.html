About Us

A Government of India Enterprise

Powering Industries Since 2009

Power Solutions is a trusted MSME-registered enterprise specializing in all kinds of LT & HT electrical works. Based in Kharar, Punjab, we have been providing comprehensive electrical solutions to industries, commercial establishments, and infrastructure projects across northern India.

Our expertise spans industrial automation, transformer repair and dehydration, ACB & VCB servicing, earthing solutions, and custom LT panel manufacturing. We pride ourselves on using only components from reputed brands such as Polycab, Havells, KEI, Bajaj Electricals, and Finolex Cables.

With a dedicated team of experienced engineers and technicians, we deliver projects on time and within budget, maintaining the highest standards of safety and quality. Our commitment to excellence has earned us the trust of over 200 clients across diverse industries.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, cost-effective, and innovative electrical solutions that power the growth of industries and communities, while maintaining the highest standards of safety, quality, and professional integrity.
